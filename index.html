Absalons Have

Info

Absalons Have takes place at a private farmhouse collective.

The residents of the place will be present, and it is very important that you respect the place and do not wander into the private areas.

Transportation & Parking

There is parking available. Follow the signs.

If you don’t use our shuttles, there is public transport about a 30-minute walk from the festival. Use Google Maps.

Camping

Follow the signs to the field, where you can set up your tent.

Camping outside of the field is not allowed.

Tickets & entry

When you arrive, go to the indoor bar and show your ticket and proof of registration to get your wristband.

Guests without wristbands will be asked to leave.

Payments

We accept MobilePay (Vipps), cash and PayPal. If you can, please pay with cash rather than PayPal.

Stamp cards are for sale at the bars and are the cheapest way to pay: a draft beer is 33 kr with a stamp card.

Dos and Don’ts

Respect the place: the residents live here. Do not wander into the private areas.

Wear your wristband. Guests without wristbands will be asked to leave.

Camp only in the field.

Please don’t bring your own alcohol, and please reuse your cups.

Food & Drinks

There will be 2 bars serving draft beer, drinks, shots and water. Please don’t bring your own alcohol. Please reuse your cups.

Breakfast/lunch is served 12–16, and a vegan dinner 18–21 (and later if needed). There are also coffee, smoothies and other things most of the day.

Toilets & Showers

There will be toilets and an outdoor shower.

Krølle-Bølle

Deep beneath the Langebjerg hill on Bornholm, the ground opens up every midnight on glowing pillars. Out steps Krølle-Bølle, a real, mischievous troll boy with a single curl on his head and tail.

He lives in the underground kingdom with his powerful father King Bobbarækus, Queen Bobbasina, and sister Borra. While his family guards their subterranean treasures, Krølle-Bølle prefers to slip away into the human world.

He roams the moonlit forests near Hammershus Castle, looking for farmers and livestock to playfully torment. The tiny troll hides milk pails, unties stable horses, and tangles the wool of sleeping sheep. If a human spots him, he instantly activates ancient underground magic to vanish into the shadows.

Locals across northern Bornholm know his tricks well and always keep a watchful eye on their belongings. They say that if your keys suddenly go missing, Krølle-Bølle is nearby laughing at your confusion.

He remains the island’s living spirit of mischief, safely returning beneath the heavy rocks before sunrise.
